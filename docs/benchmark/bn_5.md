## B5 — Measurement Reliability

Implement reliable result aggregation.

Include:

- Median and mean.
- p50, p95 and p99 where sample counts support them.
- Standard deviation.
- Confidence intervals where appropriate.
- Relative variability.
- Outlier investigation.
- Repeated independent runs.

Record CPU model, OS, runtime version, commit SHA, dataset version and configuration.

Use interleaved comparisons where appropriate.

Do not treat a tiny timing difference as a statistically meaningful improvement.

Do not remove outliers automatically without recording and justifying the methodology.
